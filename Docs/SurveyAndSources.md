# Survey and Source Policy

Krishna Mandir and major site layout may be called survey-derived only after written permission grants access and the right to create and publicly stream a derivative real-time model.

Potential source holders identified during planning:

- Geo 3D Modelling Krishna Mandir TLS project: https://geo3dmodelling.com.np/portfolio/3d-modelling-of-krishna-mandir/
- CyArk Patan Durbar Square project: https://www.cyark.org/projects/patan-durbar/resource
- Kathmandu Valley Preservation Trust: https://www.kvptnepal.org/
- Nepal Department of Archaeology through an approved source agreement

Permission must cover source access, derived-model rights, public Web delivery, commercial use, required attribution, raw-data storage and redistribution, and any duration or revocation terms.

Public dimensions and OSM may support a provisional greybox but do not establish survey accuracy. Restricted scans, plans, and photographs belong outside Git. The optimized game mesh must be validated against named control points while the raw scan or conservation mesh remains non-shipping.
